## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Have an AU playing in your head? Idol Chat Maker turns it into a realistic chat story in minutes, then exports it as an image you can post anywhere.

✨ BUILD A CHAT IN A FEW TAPS
• Pick who is talking in the bar at the bottom, type, done
• One-on-one or group chats, with sender names shown automatically
• Text, voice messages with a waveform, photos and narration
• One-tap narration: “Seen”, “Next day”, “3 days later”, “Typing…”
• Tap any message to edit it, change the speaker, reorder or delete it, with Undo

🎬 REPLAY IT LIKE A REAL CHAT
• Tap-to-Chat mode: every tap shows a “typing…” bubble, then the message pops in
• Typing time follows the length of each message
• Turn on Auto-play to screen-record your story as a video

🎨 3 CHAT STYLES
• Soft pastel with rounded bubbles
• Dark with neon outlines
• OLED black with a sunset gradient
Switch styles at any time with one tap.

🖼️ EXPORT AS A LONG IMAGE
• The whole conversation is drawn into one image, split into pages if it gets long
• Saved to your gallery, ready to share
• Every image carries a “fictional content” note so nobody is misled

⚡ START FAST WITH TEMPLATES
Not sure where to begin? Pick a ready-made story such as “Good night”, “After the concert” or “Secret message” to get a complete chat instantly, then make it your own.

👥 YOUR OWN CAST
• Choose each character’s name, colour and avatar from your photos
• Mark one character as “me” so their messages sit on the right

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works 100% offline, no account needed
• No Internet permission, no storage permission
• Your stories and photos stay on your phone

Note: Idol Chat Maker is a fan-fiction creation tool. It ships no images or real names of any artist and is not affiliated with any artist, entertainment company or social platform. You are responsible for the content you create; please do not use the app to impersonate or mislead about real people.

🌏 Available in 12 languages: English, Tiếng Việt, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 한국어, 日本語, हिन्दी, ไทย, Bahasa Indonesia, Español, Português and Français. The app follows your phone’s language.
